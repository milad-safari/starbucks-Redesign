Our Story

Rooted in the
forest floor

Brew & Botanicals began with a simple conviction: exceptional coffee is grown, not just roasted. We partner directly with smallholder farmers in cloud forests across three continents, paying four times the commodity price and investing in reforestation with every bag sold.

Our roastery in Portland is open to visitors every Saturday — come watch, smell, taste, and understand where your morning ritual truly begins.
