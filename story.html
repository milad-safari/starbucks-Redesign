"Coffee doesn't begin in the roastery. It begins in the cloud forest, with the farmer, with the rain."

The Beginning

Two friends,
one altitude

In 2004, two friends — a botanist and a barista — drove a beaten Land Rover up the cloud forests of Ethiopia's Sidama zone. They weren't looking for business opportunities. They were chasing a cup of coffee they'd heard described in a letter from a researcher at Addis Ababa University.

What they found changed everything. Not just the flavour — extraordinary, like nothing from a roastery — but the people growing it. Smallholder families living at 2100 metres, tending trees that had been in their families for generations, unable to access any market beyond the commodity exchange floor where prices were dictated by brokers thousands of miles away.

They drove back to Portland with 40 kg of green beans in the back, a handshake agreement with the Mulugeta family, and the beginnings of what would become Brew & Botanicals.

History

Twenty years of
growing together

2004

The first trip

Sarah Omondi and Luca Ferretti make their first journey to Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. First 40 kg imported.

2006

First store opens

A 30-seat café on SE Division Street, Portland, Oregon. Lines around the block within three weeks.

2010

Farmer Partnership Program

We launch FPP: guaranteed minimum pricing, annual premiums, and on-farm reforestation funding.

2014

Expanding to four continents

New sourcing partnerships in Colombia, Sumatra, and Yemen. 18 farms, 7 countries.

2018

B Corporation Certified

Independent certification confirming our supply chain, wages, and environmental commitments.

2024

320 locations, one mission

320 stores across 28 countries. Over 47 farm partners. 100,000 trees planted through the Canopy program.
